    The studio

    We only just started — and that is the whole point

    NG Hive is a small independent studio. No legacy code, no five-stage approvals, no six-month roadmaps nobody believes in. An idea from the morning is running on a real phone by the evening — and if it feels wrong in the hand, we throw it away and try again tomorrow.

    🐝

    One app — one job

    A collage app makes collages. An alarm wakes you up. We would rather ship a small thing that works than a big one with a settings screen nobody survives.

    🔒

    Your data stays with you

    Our apps have no servers and no sign-ups. Lists, photos and heart-rate readings live on the device and go nowhere else.

    📱

    Tested on real nights

    The alarm is checked against real sleep, the shopping reminder in a real parking lot. A simulator has never woken anyone up.

    ⚡

    Short iterations

    Build it, install it, live with it for a day, fix whatever annoyed us. Repeat. That loop is the entire method.

    In TestFlight

    WhatchAlarm

    A smart alarm that lives on your Apple Watch

    Instead of tearing you out of deep sleep at an exact minute, WhatchAlarm follows your heart rate in a window before your deadline and picks the lightest moment to wake you. If that moment never comes, it rings at the deadline anyway — oversleeping is not an option.

    • The whole engine runs natively on the watch — the iPhone can stay in another room
    • Wakes you with gentle haptic taps, not a siren
    • A window of up to 30 minutes before your hard deadline
    • Heart rate comes from HealthKit and is never uploaded anywhere

    ShopPing

    A shopping list that pings you when you are next to the store

    Every store you actually shop at gets its own list — a big chain or the little place around the corner you pinned on the map yourself. The moment your phone notices you are nearby and the list is not empty, it quietly reminds you. No account, no server, no subscription.

    • Geofenced reminders that keep working even when the app is closed
    • Send a list by link — whoever opens it gets the items, no account needed
    • Ready-made store lists for six countries, picked by where you actually are
    • Add your own stores by dropping a pin on the map
    • A photo and a product link for every item, drag to reorder
    • Its own font size control — readable without glasses

    H.Collage

    A collage of 2 to 10 photos, without the fuss

    Say how many photos the collage should have, pick them, and choose a grid from real previews instead of blindly cycling through layouts. Every photo can be dragged and pinched inside its own cell until it sits exactly right. Then save it to Photos or share it straight away.

    • Up to four grid variants for every photo count
    • Pan and zoom inside a cell, long-press to swap a photo
    • An adjustable frame between the photos
    • Save to Photos or share in a single tap

    Sparrows

    Family tasks that actually get done

    Parents and children give each other tasks, and the app keeps reminding whoever has to do it until the job is finished. A photo proves it, a parent accepts it, and points turn into rewards the family invented itself.

    • Reminders repeat until the task is done and go quiet during each person's own quiet hours
    • Photo proof and a parent's approval whenever a task needs checking
    • Points that turn into rewards the family agreed on
    • Private by default — a task is between the two people in it

    How we work

    From a note on a napkin to the App Store

    1. 01

      The annoyance

      Every app starts with something that genuinely annoyed us in ordinary life.

    2. 02

      The smallest version

      We build the smallest thing that can prove the idea. Usually days, not months.

    3. 03

      Real devices

      It goes onto a real phone and a real watch immediately. A simulator has never woken anyone up.

    4. 04

      TestFlight

      Testers live with it for a week, we listen to the complaints and fix exactly those.

    5. 05

      Release — and onwards

      A release is not the finish line. Updates keep coming while people keep using it.

    FAQ

    Short answers

    Who is behind NG Hive?

    An independent development studio registered in the United States. The team is small and contact is direct — you write to us, and we answer ourselves, not a support bot.

    Where can I download the apps?

    ShopPing is already on the App Store — search for «ShopPing Nearby». WhatchAlarm is available through TestFlight for now, and H.Collage is coming soon. Write to us and we will send a TestFlight invite.

    Do the apps collect my data?

    No. There is no backend at all: lists, photos and health readings never leave the device. Store search and maps use public services without any personal identifiers.

    Do you take custom projects?

    Sometimes — if the idea fits the hive. Send us a couple of sentences about what you need.

    Why a hive?

    Because a hive is many small, identical, honest cells that together hold something valuable. That is exactly how we would like our apps to be built.
